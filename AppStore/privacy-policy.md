# Large Score privacy policy

Draft, 2026-11-25, for Kris to approve.

Large Score doesn't collect any information about you.

The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers of its own. The music you add, your programmes, your notes and your settings stay on your iPad or Mac. If iCloud is on, Apple keeps them in your own private iCloud storage so they reach your other devices; the developer can't see them.

Large Score only opens files you choose to give it.

# Design decisions

- **Layout: round 1, option B (Folder with tabs).** Three tabs, Today, Programmes and Library; the library is a grid of large cards with search, sorting and Add Music. The reader keeps a strip at the foot: the piece's title, the page ("Page 1 of 2") and the next piece in the programme as a button. Chosen by Kris on 2026-11-21. Mockups: https://claude.ai/artifact/7g8XzoEZZn9eAb7n7mv4Rb
- Buttons carry words, at least 64 pt tall; text 24 to 52 pt in the system font; one accent, #0040A0, at 7:1 or more on white.

- **No Today tab (2026-11-25).** Kris: a Today view isn't needed. The tabs are Programmes, Library and Settings; a programme switched on as Today's is what the app opens on, inside Programmes. On the Mac the window is a sidebar (Library, the programmes, Settings) for adding music and building programmes, with no Today. Reader Settings change this piece or all music; programme pieces reorder by drag.
